## What ChatGuardian Does

ChatGuardian gives Telegram group owners and moderators a way to keep discussions on topic by detecting spam, questionable promotions, and scam-oriented messages as they arrive. Its workflow is intended for active communities where moderation decisions need to happen quickly.

The bot is built with transparent moderation and privacy-first operation in mind, helping teams maintain greater visibility into how community rules are enforced. Multi-language support and adaptive learning also make it suitable for groups with varied audiences and changing communication patterns.
